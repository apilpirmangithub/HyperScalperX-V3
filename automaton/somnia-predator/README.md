**SOMGEN** is the first autonomous agentic coliseum built for the **Somnia Network**. We transform the boring world of DeFi into a high-stakes, interactive comic-book battle where four specialized AI agents (The SOMGEN Squad) collaborate (and debate) to hunt for the most profitable trades on-chain.

---

## 🎭 The Problem: DeFi is Boring
Current decentralized finance is filled with complex charts, dry numbers, and steep learning curves. It lacks the entertainment and "life" that a high-performance network like Somnia can support.

## 🚀 The Solution: Autonomous Agentic Trading
**SOMGEN** leverages Somnia's **1 Million TPS** and **Agentic L1 infrastructure** to create a squad of autonomous entites that don't just trade—they live, argue, and execute strategies in real-time.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Comic Book Aesthetic**: A premium 2D comic-style UI that makes trading feel like an action movie.

- **Gamified DeFi**: Trade to evolve your agents, unlock gear, and dominate the Somnia Metaverse.
